Seat and venue of arbitration in the Supreme Court of India, 2021 to 2026

Ten reasoned judgments, decided between 1 September 2021 and 31 August 2026, on one question: where is an arbitration legally seated, and so which court supervises it? We read and coded each one and traced how the Court's answer has changed.

How the doctrine moved

Read in date order, the ten judgments fall into three phases. The coding behind this is in the Issue and Holding columns of the dataset.

2022 · HOLDING THE LINE AFTER BGS SOMA
  • Ravi Ranjan: a clause on where the tribunal "sits" names a venue, not a seat. A High Court with no territorial link cannot appoint, and consent cannot cure that.
  • BBR (India): a seat fixed by the tribunal under s 20(2) is static. Moving the hearings moves only the venue.
  • Mahakali Foods: for MSME suppliers, the MSMED Act displaces the contractual forum altogether.
2024 TO 2025 · ARIF AZIM AND THE CROSS-BORDER CASES
  • Arif Azim (3 judges): one named venue, plus chosen curial rules and nothing pointing the other way, is the seat. Dubai, so no Part I.
  • Disortho: the same method in the other direction. Bogota was never chosen as seat, Indian law governs the arbitration agreement, so Indian courts appoint.
  • Balaji Steel: "will take place in Benin" makes Benin the seat. Later contracts do not reopen it.
  • Activitas: where the clause is silent, an exclusive jurisdiction clause fixes the seat.
  • Harcharan Dass Gupta: the statute again overrides an agreed seat for MSME suppliers.
2026 · CONSOLIDATION
  • J&K ERA v Rash Builders: sets out the principles. The seat comes from the agreement and stays fixed. Hearings elsewhere, or a different place recited in the award, do not move it.
  • Bhartia Infra: twin seats plus an exclusive jurisdiction clause. The jurisdiction clause decides, and Arif Azim's forum non conveniens route is not needed.

Trends

From labels to the whole agreement

In 2022 the Court mostly policed the words "seat" and "venue". From 2024 it reads the whole contract (governing law, curial rules, jurisdiction clauses) to find what the parties meant. The word used in the clause now matters less than the agreement as a whole.

Exclusive jurisdiction clauses now act as seat clauses

Two of the last four judgments (Activitas 2025, Bhartia 2026) treat an exclusive jurisdiction clause as identifying the seat. This continues the Brahmani River Pellets and Indus Mobile line and is now the main tool for clauses that name no seat, or name two.

A new anchor precedent

BGS SGS Soma is the case leaned on most (2022, 2024, 2026). Arif Azim, itself built on BGS Soma, became the lead authority within months and is the main precedent in two later judgments. Chart 3 shows this shift.

No thumb on the scale

Five appeals or petitions succeeded and five failed. The Court sent cross-border disputes abroad as readily as it kept them in India (Arif Azim and Balaji out, Disortho in). That fits the territorial approach of BALCO more than a pro-India or pro-appellant tendency.

Statute beats party autonomy in one field

The only cases where the parties' chosen forum lost outright are the MSMED Act cases (Mahakali Foods, Harcharan Dass Gupta). Everywhere else the parties' choice, once found, controls.

The ten judgments

Method and limits

Court: Supreme Court of India. Window: 1 September 2021 to 31 August 2026. Included: reasoned judgments or reasoned orders that decide where an arbitration is seated, or which court or forum has territorial control over it because of the seat. Excluded: orders without reasons, decisions outside the window and cases on other jurisdiction questions (for example s 29A extensions or limitation). Each judgment was coded once against the Dataset template. Pick-list values follow the template's vocabularies. "Company" includes partnership and proprietorship firms.

Ten judgments cannot show a statistical trend. The counts describe this set only. Two judgments decided just after the window, Mahanadi Coalfields (23 September 2026) and ARCIS Clean Energy (12 October 2026), follow the same line but are not counted.

Data: data.xlsx and data.csv in this repository. Links in the cards point to the Supreme Court's own copies where we could locate them. Built for the Option 2 assignment, Symbiosis Law School.
